# How to contribute to this repository?

Contributions are always welcome!
:thumbsup: :tada: :heart_eyes: :100:
These can include small corrections, clarifications, improved instructions, etc.

New contributions are only accepted in the form of a **pull request**.
If you feel something should be improved, but are not sure how,
it may be more appropriate to open an **issue** instead.
After some discussion in the issue, things may become clearer and you can still open a pull request.

Formatting of most files is automatically checked and corrected
with the [pre-commit](https://pre-commit.com) program.
Run `pre-commit install` once in your clone to enable these checks for every commit.

Use semantic line breaks in Markdown files, e.g. put every sentence on its own line
and break lines at (or before) 100 characters at grammatical boundaries.

Thanks!
